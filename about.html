MEET THE FOUNDER

Aisha Habu

Founder of DeenGurl

Welcome! I am Aisha Habu, the founder of DeenGurl. I created this platform with a vision of combining Islamic knowledge, personal growth and education in one meaningful space.

My Story

DeenGurl was created from a desire to make learning, self-improvement and Islamic knowledge part of everyday life. I believe that there is always something new to learn and that personal growth is a continuous journey.

I wanted to create a platform where young people, especially girls, can explore their faith, develop useful skills, gain knowledge and find encouragement to become better individuals.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide beneficial Islamic knowledge, educational resources and personal development content that encourages young people to learn, grow and live with purpose.

Our Vision

To build a positive learning community where faith, knowledge and personal development come together, inspiring individuals to make meaningful contributions to their families and society.
