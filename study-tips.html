🎓 Study Tips

Effective studying is not only about spending many hours with your books. It is about understanding, practising and developing good learning habits.

1. Create a Study Timetable

Plan your subjects and assign time to each one. Follow a realistic schedule.

2. Understand Before Memorising

Try to understand a topic before memorising important facts and definitions.

3. Practise Past Questions

Answer past examination questions to become familiar with different question styles.

4. Take Short Notes

Write down important points in simple language to make revision easier.

5. Avoid Distractions

Choose a suitable study environment and keep unnecessary distractions away.

6. Test Yourself

Close your books and try to explain what you have learnt. This helps you identify topics that need more attention.
