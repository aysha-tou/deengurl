Qur'an & Knowledge

The Importance of Seeking Knowledge in Islam

Knowledge is very important in Islam. It helps Muslims understand their religion, distinguish right from wrong and live according to the teachings of Allah.

Seeking beneficial knowledge should be a continuous part of a Muslim's life. We should learn with sincerity and use what we learn to improve ourselves and benefit others.

Qur'an 20:114

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

"My Lord, increase me in knowledge."

Lessons to remember

  • Always seek beneficial knowledge.
  • Practise what you learn.
  • Share useful knowledge with others.
  • Ask Allah to increase you in knowledge.
Hadith & Good Deeds

Understanding Sadaqah

Sadaqah means voluntary charity. It is an act of kindness performed to please Allah. It is not limited to giving money. Helping someone, speaking kindly and offering useful assistance are also examples of good deeds.

Hadith – Sahih al-Bukhari 6021

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught that every good deed is charity.

Ways to practise Sadaqah

  • Help someone who needs assistance.
  • Give charity according to your ability.
  • Speak kindly to people.
  • Share beneficial knowledge.
Qur'anic Reflection

The Importance of Patience

Patience, known as Sabr in Arabic, is an important quality in the life of a Muslim. It teaches us to remain steadfast, trust Allah and respond wisely during difficult situations.

Qur'an 2:153

"Indeed, Allah is with the patient."

How to practise patience

  • Remember Allah during difficult moments.
  • Pray and ask Allah for guidance.
  • Avoid responding to situations with anger.
  • Remain hopeful and continue doing what is right.
