📚 General Knowledge

Knowledge helps us understand the world around us. Explore interesting facts and learn something new every day.

1. Science

Science helps us understand nature, living things, energy and the world through observation and experiments.

2. Geography

Geography is the study of the Earth, its physical features, countries, people and environments.

3. History

History teaches us about past events, important people and how societies have changed over time.

4. Health and Environment

Good hygiene, clean surroundings and healthy habits contribute to a better quality of life.

5. Learning Every Day

Ask questions, read useful materials and remain curious. Every day is an opportunity to gain new knowledge.
