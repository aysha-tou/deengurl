💻 Technology

Technology plays an important role in modern life. It helps us communicate, learn, solve problems and create useful things.

1. What Is Technology?

Technology is the use of knowledge, tools and systems to solve problems and make tasks easier.

2. Introduction to Coding

Coding is the process of writing instructions that tell computers what to do. HTML, CSS and JavaScript are examples of web technologies.

3. Digital Skills

Digital skills include using computers, creating documents, researching information and communicating online.

4. Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) allows computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

5. Online Safety

Protect your passwords, respect other people's privacy and think carefully before sharing information online.
