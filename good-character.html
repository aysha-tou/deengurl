💚 Good Character

Good character is an important part of a meaningful life. It teaches us to treat others with respect, kindness and honesty. Islam encourages beautiful manners in our daily activities.

1. Honesty

Always speak the truth and be trustworthy. Honesty builds confidence and strong relationships.

2. Patience

Learn to remain calm during difficulties and respond to situations with wisdom.

3. Kindness

Show kindness through your words, actions and treatment of other people.

4. Respect

Respect your parents, teachers, elders, friends and everyone around you.

5. Humility

Be willing to learn, accept correction and appreciate the good in others.
